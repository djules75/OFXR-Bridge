# OFXR Bridge v0.2.9.1 (V401)

Built on
[tig3rmast3r's v0.2.1](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/OFXR-Bridge/releases).
This release brings **3X Frame Gen** to OFXR.

## What's new

- **3X Frame Gen.** OFXR can now generate two frames for every frame the
  game renders, instead of one. Your game runs at a third of your headset's
  refresh rate (30 FPS at 90 Hz) and you still see the full refresh rate.
  This is for games that can't hold half your refresh rate: if a game
  already makes 45 FPS at 90 Hz, the normal 2X mode looks better.

  How to use it:

  - Tick **3X Frame Gen [live change]** in the tray menu.
  - **The switch is live:** toggle it while you play, no need to restart the
    game, the VR session or OFXR. You will see one short hitch at the
    switch.
  - **Prefer FPS over latency must be on when you start the game** (it is on
    by default). Ticking 3X also ticks it and greys it out, because 3X needs
    it. If the game you are playing was started with it off, 3X cannot
    switch live: a **"game restart needed"** window opens on your desktop
    (look at your monitor), and 3X starts the next time you launch the game.

### What to expect

It works very well, and is completely playable. Tested in Microsoft Flight
Simulator 2024, The Callisto Protocol, Cyberpunk 2077 and No Man's Sky.

- **More artefacts than OFXR's normal 2X mode.** Two generated frames in a
  row have more to guess than one.
- **Fewer artefacts than SteamVR Motion Smoothing at 3X.**
- **Artefacts show mostly in sideways motion**, such as strafing or flying
  past something close.

### Latency

On top of the game running at a lower frame rate, 3X adds a little latency
compared with 2X with **Prefer FPS over latency**. 

OFXR tunes this by itself while you play: give it about 15 seconds after
starting or after switching to 3X to settle. Head rotation is corrected by
SteamVR at display time in every mode, so looking around feels the same.

## Known issues

- **D3D11 games need the D3D11 bridge, which is on by default.** If you
  turned it off (`d3d11_bridge=0` in `tray.ini`), those games stay at 2X.
  D3D12 and Vulkan games are covered.
- **SteamVR: turn off Motion Smoothing and "fixed frame rate at half" for
  the game** in SteamVR's per-application video settings. They fight with
  OFXR's own pacing and look like an OFXR problem.
- **Pick a refresh rate that matches your game.** 2X wants about half your
  refresh rate, 3X about a third. The FPS number shows when the game can't
  keep up.
- **No FPS overlay in Vulkan games.** Vulkan games have no reliable FPS
  reading yet.

## Installing

Unzip anywhere and run `OFXRBridgeTray.exe`. It arms the bridge by itself.
Keep `OFXRBridgeTray.exe` and the `ofxr` folder together; it holds the OpenXR
layer, its settings and the Vulkan layer. If an earlier version is armed,
disarm it and close its tray first.


Please report results, working or not, on this fork's
[GitHub Issues](../../issues), with an OFXR flight log where you can. To
record one, enable **Bridge flight recorder** in the tray, reproduce the
problem, then use **Open bridge logs**. Logs are written under
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OFXR Bridge\RuntimeLayer\v401\`.
