# OFXR Bridge v0.2.13.1 (V433)

**A fix release on top of v0.2.12.2.** On Virtual Desktop, games that had
higher latency and frame drops since v0.2.12.1 are back to the way
v0.2.11.2 ran them, and on SteamVR the short frame-rate dips that followed
a hiccup are now a single repeated frame. Everything else is v0.2.12.2.

Built on
[tig3rmast3r's v0.2.1](https://github.com/tig3rmast3r/OFXR-Bridge/releases).

> [!WARNING]
> **Pimax headsets: use SteamVR, not Pimax OpenXR.** Pimax Play's OpenXR
> runtime keeps an extra copy in VRAM of every image it is handed, so OFXR
> costs about **1.5 times** more on it than on SteamVR, and the copies stay
> until the game closes. We have reported it to Pimax so they can fix their
> runtime; there is nothing OFXR can do about it in the meantime. Until it is
> fixed, run your Pimax through SteamVR with
> [sboys3's native SteamVR driver](https://store.pimax.com/blogs/blogs/sboys3-native-steamvr-driver-setup-guide)
> and make SteamVR the active OpenXR runtime (SteamVR → Settings → OpenXR).
> This matters most in MSFS 2024, which is already close to the VRAM limit
> at Pimax resolutions. Details in the
> [troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/djules75/OFXR-Bridge/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#how-much-vram-ofxr-uses).

## 🛠️ Not working, or not feeling smoother?

### ➡️ [Read the troubleshooting guide first](https://github.com/djules75/OFXR-Bridge/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md)

It checks in one step whether OFXR is running in your game, then lists the

## New in v0.2.13.1

### Virtual Desktop: the latency and frame drops since v0.2.12.1 are gone

Reported with IL-2 Great Battles on a Quest 3: total latency went from a
steady 47 ms on v0.2.11.2 to 58 to 70 ms on v0.2.12.1 and v0.2.12.2, with
visible frame drops.

OFXR sends Virtual Desktop two frames for every frame the game renders,
the generated one and then the real one, and they have to arrive one
refresh apart. v0.2.12.1 changed how games like IL-2 are run on Virtual
Desktop, and on that path the real frame followed the generated one by
only 3 ms, on every frame. Virtual Desktop then had two frames for one
refresh, which is where the latency and the drops came from.

Games whose frame loop runs on a single thread, which is most of them, now
get the v0.2.11.2 mode back on Virtual Desktop, chosen once when the game
starts and kept for the whole session. The reporter's log on this build
shows the same game running with nine times fewer repeated frames than
on v0.2.11.2, and he reports it "just like 416, even better". Games that
run their frame loop on two threads, such as No Man's Sky, keep the
v0.2.12.1 behaviour, which is the right one for them. Nothing changes on
SteamVR or Pimax OpenXR.

### SteamVR: shorter dips after a hiccup

Reported in MSFS 2024 with 3X Frame Gen at 72 Hz, on a Pimax through
SteamVR: short frame-rate dips of one to three seconds.

After a small hiccup, SteamVR starts showing every frame one refresh late,
and OFXR took about twenty frames to move its timing back into step. Those
twenty frames were the dip. OFXR now makes the move in one go, which costs
a single repeated frame, and if the next hiccup arrives before the
correction has settled it is handled the same way instead of being walked
off again. SteamVR only.

## Known issues

- **Your game has to reach half your headset's refresh rate, with some
  headroom** (45 fps at 90 Hz, 50 at 100 Hz), for every frame to come out
  evenly. Generating frames costs GPU time too: No Man's Sky ran at 94 fps
  without OFXR on our test machine and reached an even 100 at 100 Hz, but
  113 to 115 at 120 Hz. If your number stops short of the refresh rate,
  choose a lower refresh rate, or lower the per-eye resolution or the
  optical-flow resolution. The
  [troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/djules75/OFXR-Bridge/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#c-ofxr-works-but-it-does-not-feel-better)
  has the details.
- **Pausing is not the same as disarming.** A paused game still runs through
  OFXR. To rule OFXR out of a problem, disarm it and restart the game.
- **Check your real per-eye resolution.** SteamVR's global resolution and a
  per-game resolution **multiply**: 200% global with 200% for the game gives
  four times the pixels, and OFXR's memory grows with them. The
  troubleshooting guide explains how to read the real value from a flight
  log.
- **SteamVR: turn off Motion Smoothing and "fixed frame rate at half" for
  the game** in SteamVR's per-application video settings. They fight with
  OFXR's own pacing.
- **No FPS overlay in Vulkan games**, so no pause symbol there either. The
  tray entry still shows whether generation is paused.

## Installing

Unzip anywhere and run `OFXRBridgeTray.exe`. It arms the bridge by itself.
Keep `OFXRBridgeTray.exe` and the `ofxr` folder together; it holds the OpenXR
layer, its settings and the Vulkan layer. If an earlier version is armed,
disarm it and close its tray first.

Please report results, working or not, on this fork's
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/djules75/OFXR-Bridge/issues), with an OFXR flight log where you can. To
record one, enable **Bridge flight recorder** in the tray, reproduce the
problem, then use **Open bridge logs**. Logs are written under
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OFXR Bridge\RuntimeLayer\v433\`. Read the
[troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/djules75/OFXR-Bridge/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) first: most problems are
solved there in a few minutes.
